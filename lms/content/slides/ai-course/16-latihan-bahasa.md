# 🌍 Latihan Modul 16: Bahasa Asing dengan AI

**Durasi Total:** 60–80 menit
**Tools yang Dibutuhkan:** ChatGPT/Claude, Google Translate, Grammarly (opsional)

---

## 🎯 Tujuan Latihan

Setelah menyelesaikan latihan ini, kamu akan mampu:
- Berlatih percakapan bahasa Inggris dengan AI
- Mengoreksi grammar dengan bantuan AI
- Membangun kosakata baru secara sistematis
- Meningkatkan kemampuan menulis dengan feedback AI

---

## Exercise 1: Conversation Practice 10 Menit ⭐⭐

**⏱️ Waktu: 15 menit (10 menit bicara + 5 menit review)**

### Instruksi

Buka ChatGPT dan mulai percakapan dalam bahasa Inggris tentang topik sehari-hari.

### Prompt untuk AI

```
Let's practice English conversation. You are a friendly barista 
at a coffee shop. I'm a customer. 

Rules:
1. Speak only in English
2. After 10 exchanges, give me feedback on my grammar, 
   vocabulary, and fluency
3. Rate my conversation 1-10 and suggest 3 improvements
4. Highlight any mistakes I made with corrections

Start the conversation now. Greet me as a customer.
```

### Topik yang Bisa Dipilih

| Level | Topik | Contoh Awal |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| ⭐ | Belanja | "Hi, I'd like to buy..." |
| ⭐ | Cuaca | "What's the weather like today?" |
| ⭐⭐ | Perjalanan | "I need directions to..." |
| ⭐⭐ | Interview kerja | "Tell me about yourself" |
| ⭐⭐⭐ | Debates | "I believe that social media..." |

### Contoh Percakapan

```
AI: Good morning! Welcome to The Cozy Cup. What can I get 
    for you today?

You: Hi, I want coffee... err, I want to order a latte, 
     please. Medium size.

AI: Sure thing! Would you like any flavor in your latte? 
    We have vanilla, caramel, and hazelnut.

You: Hmm, what do you recommend?

AI: Our caramel latte is very popular! Would you like to 
    try that?

You: Yes, please. And... how much is it?

AI: That would be $4.50. Anything else?

You: No, that's all. Thank you!
```

### Review Setelah Percakapan

Setelah 10 exchanges, minta AI memberikan:

```
Now give me feedback:
1. My grammar mistakes (with corrections)
2. New vocabulary I could use
3. More natural phrasing suggestions
4. Overall fluency rating (1-10)
5. One thing to practice next time
```

### 💡 Tips
- **Jangan gunakan Google Translate** saat chatting — biarkan alami!
- Jika tidak tahu kata, gunakan deskripsi: "the hot brown drink" (bukan langsung translate)
- Print screen hasil feedback untuk portofolio belajar

## Exercise 2: Grammar Correction ⭐⭐

**⏱️ Waktu: 15 menit**

### Instruksi

Tulis 5 paragraf pendek (masing-masing 3-5 kalimat) tentang aktivitas sehari-hari, lalu minta AI mengoreksi grammar-nya.

### Contoh Paragraf yang Ditulis

```
Paragraph 1:
Yesterday I go to school by motorcycle. The weather is very 
hot but I enjoy it. My teacher give us a test in mathematics. 
I think I do well in the test.

Paragraph 2:
Last week, I and my friend go to mall. We buying some clothes 
and eating lunch at food court. The food was very delicious. 
I was so happy that day.

Paragraph 3:
Every morning, I wake up at 5 o'clock. I brush my teeth and 
take a shower. After that, I eating breakfast with my family. 
Then I go to school by bus.

Paragraph 4:
My hobby is reading book. I reading every night before sleep. 
I like fiction story because it very interesting. Last month 
I read 5 books already.

Paragraph 5:
Last holiday, my family and I going to Bali. We stay there for 
5 days. We visiting many temple and beach. The view was so 
beautiful and I want to go back there again.
```

### Prompt untuk AI

```
Here are 5 paragraphs I wrote in English. Please:

1. CORRECT all grammar mistakes (show original → corrected)
2. EXPLAIN each correction briefly
3. Give a GRADE for each paragraph (A-F)
4. Write ONE "improved" version of my worst paragraph
5. Give me a summary of my most common mistakes

Paragraphs:
[paste 5 paragraf di atas]
```

### Contoh Output yang Diharapkan

```
Paragraph 1 Corrections:
✗ "I go" → ✓ "I went" (past tense)
✗ "is very hot" → ✓ "was very hot" (past tense consistency)
✗ "give" → ✓ "gave" (past tense)
✗ "I do well" → ✓ "I did well" (past tense)
Grade: C+ (mostly tense errors)

Paragraph 2 Corrections:
✗ "I and my friend" → ✓ "My friend and I" (word order)
✗ "go to mall" → ✓ "went to the mall" (article + tense)
✗ "buying...eating" → ✓ "bought...ate" (past tense)

MOST COMMON MISTAKE: Past tense (Simple Past) — 12 errors
RECOMMENDATION: Review irregular past tense verbs
```

### 💡 Tips
- **Tulis dulu TANPA bantuan AI** — ini latihan untuk mengidentifikasi kesalahanmu sendiri
- Setelah koreksi AI, coba perbaiki paragraf lainnya tanpa bantuan
- Simpan koreksi sebagai "cheat sheet" kesalahan grammar-mu

## Exercise 3: Vocabulary Builder ⭐

**⏱️ Waktu: 10 menit**

### Instruksi

Minta AI generate daftar kosakata baru dalam topik tertentu.

### Prompt untuk AI

```
Create a vocabulary list of 20 English words related to 
"Technology and Gadgets" for intermediate learners.

For each word provide:
1. The word
2. Pronunciation guide (simplified)
3. Indonesian translation
4. Part of speech (noun/verb/adj)
5. Example sentence
6. Synonym (similar meaning)

Format as a table. Include words from easy to hard.
```

```
| 2 | Download | DOWN-load | Mengunduh | verb | I need to download this app |
| 3 | Upload | UP-load | Mengunggah | verb | She uploaded photos to cloud |
| 4 | Gadget | GA-jet | Gadget | noun | The latest gadget from Apple |
| 5 | Firmware | FIRM-wear | Firmware | noun | Update your firmware first |
| ... | ... | ... | ... | ... | ... |
| 18 | Cryptography | krip-TOG-ra-fi | Kriptografi | noun | Cryptography protects data |
| 19 | Algorithm | AL-go-ritm | Algoritma | noun | The algorithm sorts data fast |
```

```
Include the answer key at the end. Make the sentences 
practical and everyday.
```

## Exercise 4: Translation Challenge ⭐⭐⭐

**⏱️ Waktu: 10 menit**

### Instruksi

Terjemahkan 10 kalimat kompleks dari Bahasa Indonesia ke Inggris **tanpa bantuan**, lalu bandingkan dengan AI.

### Kalimat untuk Diterjemahkan

```
1. Meskipun cuaca tidak menentu, kami memutuskan untuk tetap 
   melanjutkan perjalanan ke puncak gunung.

2. Teknologi kecerdasan buatan telah mengubah cara kita 
   berinteraksi dengan informasi dan satu sama lain.

3. Guru yang berdedikasi selalu berusaha menemukan metode 
   pengajaran yang paling efektif untuk setiap siswa.

4. Pandemi COVID-19 memberikan dampak signifikan terhadap 
   perekonomian global dan kehidupan masyarakat.

5. Indonesia sebagai negara kepulauan terbesar di dunia memiliki 
   keanekaragaman budaya yang luar biasa.

6. Perubahan iklim menjadi ancaman serius bagi generasi mendatang 
   jika tidak segera ditangani secara kolektif.

7. Kreativitas dan inovasi adalah kunci utama dalam menghadapi 
   tantangan abad ke-21 yang semakin kompleks.

8. Akses terhadap pendidikan berkualitas merupakan hak setiap 
   warga negara tanpa memandang latar belakang sosial.

9. Media sosial memiliki dua sisi — dapat menjadi alat yang 
   bermanfaat sekaligus sumber masalah jika tidak digunakan 
   secara bijak.

10. Kolaborasi antar berbagai bidang ilmu sering menghasilkan 
    temuan yang revolusioner dan tidak terduga.
```

### Prompt untuk AI (Setelah Kamu Mencoba Sendiri)

```
Here are my translations. Compare them with the "ideal" 
translations. Rate each 1-10 and explain differences:

My translations:
1. [your translation]
2. [your translation]
...

For each sentence, provide:
1. My translation
2. Ideal translation  
3. Score (1-10)
4. Key difference explanation
5. A more natural/advanced alternative
```

### Contoh Perbandingan

```
Kalimat 1:
Kamu: "Despite the uncertain weather, we decided to continue 
       our trip to the mountain peak."
Ideal: "Despite the unpredictable weather, we decided to proceed 
        with our journey to the mountain summit."
Score: 8/10
Notes: "uncertain" → "unpredictable" lebih natural untuk cuaca
        "continue" → "proceed with" lebih formal
        "trip" → "journey" lebih cocok untuk pendakian
```

### 💡 Tips
- Jangan terlalu terpaku pada "kata per kata" — terjemahkan maknanya
- Perhatikan: idiom, collocations, dan naturalness
- Coba terjemahkan bolak-balik (Inggris→Indonesia) untuk memahami nuansa

## Exercise 5: Writing Improvement ⭐⭐

**⏱️ Waktu: 15 menit**

### Instruksi

Tulis esai pendek, minta AI improve, lalu bandingkan versi original vs improved.

### Prompt untuk AI

```
I wrote a short essay about "The Importance of Learning English". 
Here it is:

[paste esaimu]

Please:
1. IMPROVE the essay while keeping my main ideas
2. Highlight what you changed and WHY
3. Rate the original (1-10) and the improved version (1-10)
4. Give me 3 specific writing tips based on my weaknesses
5. Rewrite my INTRODUCTION paragraph in 3 different styles:
   a) Academic/formal
   b) Casual/conversational  
   c) Persuasive/emotional
```

### Contoh Perbandingan

```
ORIGINAL (Score: 5/10):
"English is important. Many people need English for work. 
Students should learn English because it useful. In the 
future, English will be more important."

IMPROVED (Score: 8/10):
"English has become an indispensable skill in today's 
globalized world. As international business continues to 
expand, proficiency in English opens doors to countless 
career opportunities. For students, mastering English is 
no longer optional — it's a necessity. Looking ahead, the 
demand for English communication skills will only continue 
to grow."

CHANGES:
- Added specific reasons (not just "important")
- Used stronger vocabulary (indispensable, proficiency)
- Added transition phrases
- More developed ideas with supporting details
- Fixed grammar ("it useful" → "it is useful")
```

### 💡 Tips
- **Bandingkan lalu tulis ulang sendiri** — jangan hanya copy improved version
- Catat phrases/structures baru yang kamu suka dari versi improved
- Coba terapkan gaya improved ke topik essay lain

## Exercise 6: TOEFL/IELTS Mock Test ⭐⭐⭐

**⏱️ Waktu: 15 menit**

### Instruksi

Minta AI membuatkan simulasi tes TOEFL/IELTS sederhana.

### Prompt untuk AI

```
Create a mini IELTS practice test with:
1. Reading section (1 passage, 5 questions)
2. Listening section (transcript + 5 questions)
3. Writing section (1 Task 2 essay prompt)
4. Speaking section (3 Part 1 questions)

Make it intermediate level. After I answer, grade my 
responses and give feedback.
```

### Contoh Reading Passage

```
PASSAGE: "The Rise of Remote Work"

The COVID-19 pandemic accelerated a trend that was already 
underway: the shift to remote work. Before 2020, only about 
5% of full-time workers in developed countries worked primarily 
from home. By mid-2020, that number had jumped to over 40%.

Companies quickly discovered that many roles could be performed 
effectively outside the traditional office. Video conferencing 
tools like Zoom saw explosive growth, with daily meeting 
participants increasing from 10 million in December 2019 to 
300 million by April 2020.

However, the transition wasn't without challenges. Many workers 
reported feelings of isolation, difficulty separating work from 
personal life, and burnout from the "always connected" culture. 
Studies showed that while individual productivity often increased, 
collaboration and innovation sometimes suffered.

Questions:
1. What percentage worked from home before 2020? (A: 5%, B: 40%, C: 50%)
2. What happened to Zoom usage? 
3. What is ONE challenge of remote work mentioned?
4. What "suffered" according to studies?
5. The word "accelerated" is closest in meaning to...?
```

### 💡 Tips
- Setelah mengerjakan, minta AI menjelaskan jawaban yang salah
- Catat jenis soal yang paling sulit untuk latihan lebih lanjut
- Set target: "Saya ingin skor IELTS X" — AI bisa bantu buat study plan

## ⚠️ Common Pitfalls

1. **Langsung pakai AI** — Latihan menulis TANPA bantuan dulu, baru bandingkan
2. **Hanya fokus grammar** — Vocabulary dan naturalness sama pentingnya
3. **Tidak review feedback** — Baca setiap koreksi dan pahami kenapa salah
4. **Tidak praktik speaking** — Coba juga rekam suara dan minta AI menilai pronunciation
5. **Bosan di tengah jalan** — Break setiap 15 menit, ambil minum dulu!
